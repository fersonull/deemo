# DEEMO v1.0.0

DEEMO helps students prepare for research defenses through a voice-based AI mock panel.

## Highlights

- **Manuscript analysis:** upload PDF or DOCX files, review editable summaries, and get a panel adapted to your research field.
- **Defense practice:** rehearse Title, Pre-Oral and Final defenses with up to three AI panelists, distinct voices, agenda-based questions, adjustable difficulty and follow-ups.
- **Live sessions:** microphone checks, voice or typed answers, transcripts, pause/resume and spoken contradiction challenges.
- **Readiness reports:** scores, priority fixes, contradictions, topic coverage and per-question feedback, with focused practice for weak topics.
- **Custom rubrics:** use the default criteria or upload your panel's rubric.
- **Group projects:** join by code, share manuscript analysis and rubrics, set individual focus areas and optionally share scores.
- **Progress tracking:** readiness trends, stage progress, session history, sorting and date filters.
- **Account controls:** email or Google sign-in, course and avatar preferences, data export and account deletion.
- **Notices:** in-app inbox and admin announcements, with background push support pending device verification.
- **Premium demo:** unlimited practice starts and manuscripts, Final Defense, up to three panelists, detailed analytics and PDF exports. No real payments.
- **Latest improvements:** instant settings feedback, keyboard-friendly Setup and loading skeletons during History filter updates.
- **Companion tools:** web admin, marketing website, interactive presentation deck and promo video project.

DEEMO supports practice and does not replace advisers or panelists. Final device and deployment verification remain pending.
