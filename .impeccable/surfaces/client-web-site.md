Scope: DEEMO public site (`client/web/site`), one long page plus `privacy.html`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: students of any course preparing a research defense (18+), usually alone at night on a mid-range Android phone; also the capstone panel and classmates evaluating the project. Action: download the Android build from https://github.com/fersonull/deemo/releases. Proof: the app's real sample paper (ReqTrack), real course → panel lineup, real rubric weights and readiness formula, real plan limits. No testimonials, counts or endorsements.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A real defense panel holds note slips of questions; DEEMO writes them from your manuscript, deals them to the right panelists, reads them aloud and scores the answers. The slips are the page's only traveling material. Refuses the SaaS landing (hero mockup + feature cards + logo strip) and any flashcard look.

OWN-WORLD: The app's paper world: #F7F3EC paper ground, ink #2A2420, dusk #5A66C7 as the slips' ruled margin and the drenched live-panel beat (duskInk #343996 ground), sand #EDE5D8 for the report, amber #EBB353 only on the contradiction slip. Slips: off-white ruled notepaper, dusk margin rule, asker and stage written on them. Bricolage Grotesque display, DM Sans body, DM Mono only for timers, weights and the formula. Deemo and the panel faces as the only characters.

STORY: The visitor sees the panel already has questions about a real paper, flicks through them, follows a slip from manuscript to panelist to spoken question to contradiction to score, sees the honest limits, and downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: Bricolage headline "Your panel already has questions." at display scale, one line of support, Download for Android (primary, ink pill) and See how it works. Right seven columns, bleeding past the column edge: a three.js stack of ruled slips, top slip legible ("Why only 30 respondents, and why purposive sampling?", Prof. Cruz, Methodology, sample paper ReqTrack), Deemo peeking over the stack, a quiet "Drag a question" hint and a Next question button. Signature interaction: drag or flick the top slip; it flips to its back (the follow-up DEEMO would ask) and settles on a discard pile while the next slip rises.

FORM: Surface structure "The question slips", position 6 on the ranked list of seven grounded structures; seed key c774c0ab (surface, persuade). Code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
Flicking the top slip off the hero stack and watching it flip to the panel's follow-up.
