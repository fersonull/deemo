BSIT Capstone Project

DEEMO

"Practice your capstone defense with an AI panel that has actually read your paper."

4B — Kimberly's Group · Pinamalayan Maritime Foundation and Technological College, Inc.

The Problem

Students can build it. Defending it out loud is another story.

  • Difficulty expressing technical ideas, answering unexpected panel questions, and justifying methodology, system design, and testing.
  • Defense anxiety — fear of negative evaluation impairs thinking on one's feet.
  • Self-review, peer practice, and short adviser consultations don't replicate a real panel's pressure or unpredictability.
  • Existing AI tools simulate generic interviews or public speaking — not an academic capstone defense.

Proposed Solution

DEEMO

  • An AI-powered mobile app that runs realistic, voice-based mock panel defenses.
  • Grounded in the student's own uploaded manuscript — not generic interview questions.
  • Covers all three defense stages: Title Defense, Pre-Oral Defense, Final Defense.
  • Unlimited practice, scored against the student's actual rubric.

Chapter III · System Overview

What DEEMO actually does

  • Primary users: 4th-year BSIT students preparing for capstone defense.
  • Secondary users: faculty advisers (review exported reports; no dedicated dashboard).
  • Unlimited, realistic, voice-based mock defenses grounded in the student's own manuscript.
  • Scored against the student's actual rubric — not a generic checklist.
  • Unlike self-review or peer practice, it recreates panel pressure and unpredictability.

Chapter III · System Objectives

What the study set out to do

  • Identify challenges in defense preparation — communication, confidence, critical thinking, answering panel questions.
  • Evaluate how existing preparation methods influence readiness.
  • Develop an AI mock panel simulator that analyzes manuscripts and generates adaptive questions.
  • Assess users' perception of usability.
  • Evaluate effectiveness in assessing communication, reasoning, and verbal–manuscript consistency.
  • Examine perceived improvement in confidence, communication, critical thinking, and readiness.

Chapter III · Operational Definition

Key terms

  • Readiness Score — a 0–100 score from the weighted rubric result, adjusted for manuscript consistency and coverage.
  • Bounded Adaptive Follow-Up — the panel asks at most a fixed number of follow-ups per topic (default 2) before moving on.
  • Live Verbal Consistency Checker — flags contradictions between what the student says and what the manuscript states.
  • Adaptive Domain Profiling — the panel's questions adjust to the student's discipline/field.
  • Ephemeral Token — a short-lived credential the server mints, locked to the model, prompt, and tools, so the phone never holds a standing AI key.
  • Stage Modes — Title Defense, Pre-Oral Defense, and Final Defense, each with a different question focus.

Tech Stack

Built on

  • Mobile: Expo (React Native, TypeScript)
  • Server: Bun + Express, domain-driven modules
  • Database: PostgreSQL via Prisma
  • Auth: Firebase Authentication (sign-in only)
  • AI — live panel: Gemini Live
  • AI — analysis & scoring: Gemini Flash fallback chain

Scope & Delimitations

What DEEMO is — and isn't

  • Android only, English only
  • Cloud AI only — no offline mode
  • Freemium is simulated — no real payments
  • Scores carry no official academic weight
  • Not a grammar or citation checker
  • Not a plagiarism detector
  • No fact-checking or emotion recognition
  • No adviser dashboard
