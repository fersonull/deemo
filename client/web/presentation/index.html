What did we set out to do?

  1. Identify the challengesCommunication, confidence, critical thinking.
  2. Evaluate current prepSelf-review, peer practice, adviser consultations.
  3. Build the AI mock panelReads manuscripts, asks adaptive questions.
  4. Assess usabilityHow students rate the app.
  5. Test the evaluationCommunication, reasoning, consistency.
  6. Measure readinessConfidence and defense readiness gains.

The terms we use.

Readiness Score
0–100 from the rubric, adjusted for consistency and coverage.
Bounded Follow-Up
At most two follow-ups per topic, then the panel moves on.
Verbal Consistency Checker
Flags when an answer contradicts the manuscript.
Adaptive Domain Profiling
Questions fit the student's field.
Ephemeral Token
A short-lived key locked to the panel. No AI key on the phone.
Stage Modes
Title, Pre-Oral, Final: each with its own question focus.
