Methodology panelist

What did you set out to do?

Unlimited, voice-based mock defenses grounded in the student's own manuscript, scored against their actual rubric.

Primary users
4th-year BSIT students preparing for capstone defense
Secondary users
Faculty advisers, through exported reports
  1. Identify challenges in defense preparation: communication, confidence, critical thinking, answering questions.
  2. Evaluate how existing preparation methods influence readiness.
  3. Develop an AI mock panel simulator that analyzes manuscripts and generates adaptive questions.
  4. Assess users' perception of usability.
  5. Evaluate effectiveness in assessing communication, reasoning, and verbal–manuscript consistency.
  6. Examine perceived improvement in confidence, communication, critical thinking, and readiness.

Methodology panelist

Define your terms.

Readiness Score
A 0–100 score from the weighted rubric result, adjusted for manuscript consistency and coverage.
Bounded Adaptive Follow-Up
The panel asks at most a fixed number of follow-ups per topic (default 2), then moves on.
Live Verbal Consistency Checker
Flags contradictions between what the student says and what the manuscript states.
Adaptive Domain Profiling
The panel's questions adjust to the student's discipline and field.
Ephemeral Token
A short-lived credential the server mints, locked to the model, prompt, and tools. The phone never holds a standing AI key.
Stage Modes
Title, Pre-Oral, and Final Defense, each with a different question focus.
