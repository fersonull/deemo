01

Upload your manuscript.

PDF or DOCX, up to 20 MB: a title proposal, Chapters 1–3, or the full paper. The file itself is thrown away after reading; only its text is kept.

02

DEEMO reads it the way a panel would.

Document Logic Analysis pulls out your problem, objectives, methodology, scope and limits, plus the checkable facts a panel could catch you contradicting. It also works out your study's field, so the panel in the next step is already yours. You can correct anything before you practice.

  • KEY FACT Thirty (30) respondents, purposive sampling. Ch. 3
  • KEY FACT Evaluated with ISO/IEC 25010, 4-point Likert scale. Ch. 3

03

A panel built for your field.

A Nursing thesis isn't defended to an engineer. Pick a course and watch the panel reassemble: this runs the same seating logic the app does.

    04

    Asked out loud. Answered out loud.

    The panel speaks, you answer by voice, and each question is labelled so you learn the pattern. Up to two follow-ups per topic, then it moves on, like a real panel. Your voice goes straight to Gemini; our server never joins the call.

    Contradict your paper, and it notices.

    Know how ready you are.
