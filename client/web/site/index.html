Sample paper

ReqTrack: An Enrollment Requirements Tracking System for PMFTCI

Chapter 1. The Problem

Students queue at the registrar to check paper requirement checklists, and staff update records by hand. This study lets students see missing requirements on their phone and lets the registrar update records in real time.

Chapter 3. Methodology

Agile (Scrum), descriptive-developmental design. 30 respondents by purposive sampling; an ISO/IEC 25010 questionnaire on a 4-point Likert scale, analyzed by weighted mean.

Chapter 4. Results

Overall weighted mean 3.42 (Very Acceptable); the lowest characteristic is Security, at 3.10.

    Questions you might have.

    Does DEEMO keep recordings of my voice?

    No. Your voice goes from your phone to the AI during the session and is never stored. Only the text transcript is kept.

    What happens to my manuscript file?

    It is read once to pull out what the panel needs, then discarded. You see that summary and can correct it before you practice.

    Who processes my work?

    Google's Gemini models run the panel and the scoring. DEEMO uses Gemini's paid tier, because the free tier allows human review of what you send.

    Can my groupmates practice on the same paper?

    Yes. Share your project's code and they can join and practice on the same manuscript. Each of you chooses whether to share your score with the group.

    Does it check grammar, citations or plagiarism?

    No. DEEMO is a mock panel: it asks, follows up and scores your answers. It doesn't edit or fact-check your paper.

    What do I need?

    An Android phone, an internet connection, and to be 18 or older. Sessions are in English.

    Can I delete everything?

    Yes. Delete my data in Profile removes your account, manuscripts, sessions and reports.
